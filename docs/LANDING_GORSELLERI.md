# LİNBA landing page görselleri

Bu görseller yerleşik imagegen aracıyla üretildi. Şeffaflık korunarak WebP biçiminde sıkıştırıldı; görsel içeriği değiştirilmedi. Sayfadaki metinler HTML olarak sunulur.

Uygulamada kullanılan dosyalar:

- `public/landing/training.webp`
- `public/landing/rehearsal.webp`
- `public/landing/follow-up.webp`

## landing-hero

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent isometric hero illustration for LİNBA, a real presentation-centered training platform.
Primary request: an exceptionally refined, restrained isometric composition showing a presentation turning into an interactive training. One main widescreen presentation surface, a small aligned stack of two slide sheets behind it, and one slim participant phone standing at lower right. On the presentation, an elegant simple diagram of three linked geometric forms; on the phone, two simple conversation shapes and a small document-source thumbnail. No readable text. A tiny mint participation response tile sits near the phone. Only these objects, arranged as one coherent architectural composition, no scattered confetti.
Style: precise contemporary editorial 3D, orthographic isometric camera, engineered thin planes and crisp bevels, matte white porcelain and charcoal edges, tasteful subtle paper grain, soft directional studio shadows. Think crafted physical presentation objects, not shiny tech toys. Strong visual hierarchy, beautiful negative space around objects; no framing card, no platform pedestal.
Palette: predominantly neutral white and soft cool gray; carefully limited lilac #AE8CE0 on slide planes, deep fuchsia #E80076 for a small content accent, fresh mint #39DEC0 for the response tile. No blue. No purple glow.
Composition: landscape 4:3 artwork with generous clear outer margins, objects occupy about 80% of the canvas, center of gravity slightly right, all objects fully visible. Designed to sit beside real headline text in HTML. The image itself contains no words, letters, logos, captions, or UI chrome.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, only subtle object contact shadows. Must work over both white and deep charcoal webpages.
Avoid: gradients as backdrop, neon, bloom, halos, robot, brain, magic sparkles, floating meaningless orbs, faces, cartoon people, glassmorphism, glossy jelly, thick rounded clay objects, a whole webpage screenshot, a laptop keyboard, tiny illegible text.
```

## landing-rehearsal

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: supporting transparent isometric illustration for LİNBA voice rehearsal.
Primary request: a quietly sophisticated isometric still life of voice rehearsal before a training. A thin upright rectangular presentation sheet with one simple lilac diagram, three small elegant profile cards beside it representing different fictional participants with restrained abstract portrait silhouettes, and a low horizontal mint audio waveform strip linking them. A single subtle fuchsia speech shape beside the nearest participant card. No words. These four-to-five closely grouped elements form one cohesive physical object arrangement, not a cloud of UI widgets.
Style: high-end precise editorial 3D, orthographic isometric view, matte white surfaces, charcoal thin edges, lilac paper layers, crisp small bevels, very soft studio contact shadows, restrained premium product illustration. No gummy clay aesthetic. Match a white/lilac/fuchsia/mint presentation product.
Palette: white and cool neutral gray dominate, selective lilac #AE8CE0, mint #39DEC0, tiny fuchsia #E80076 accents. No blue and no luminous effects.
Composition: landscape 4:3, fully visible grouped objects occupying about 75% of canvas with ample transparent margins. Clear readable silhouette at 420px wide.
Background: true transparent alpha suitable for white and charcoal webpage surfaces.
Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no logo, no whole webpage, no numbers, no robot, no brain, no sparkle, no neon, no glow, no background gradient, no glassmorphism, no platform pedestal, no exaggerated rounded clay toy characters.
```

## landing-follow-up

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent supporting isometric illustration for LİNBA's post-training document assistant and report.
Primary request: a small cohesive isometric still life: two thin white presentation document sheets stacked at rear left, a slim white portrait conversation panel forward right with three simple dark-gray and pale-lilac conversation shapes and tiny source-document attachment, and one narrow report sheet with exactly three understated mint/fuchsia vertical chart bars at lower left. Thin lilac connector line ties the conversation source thumbnail to the document sheets, expressing answers grounded in the presentation. Objects arranged close together as a precise architectural composition with abundant outer breathing room.
Style: sophisticated editorial 3D product illustration, orthographic isometric camera, matte porcelain-white planes with charcoal fine edges, crisp thin bevels and slight paper texture; sparse lilac paper accents #AE8CE0, tiny fuchsia #E80076, mint #39DEC0. Elegant physical stationery-like geometry, not a toy and not a fake webpage screenshot.
Composition landscape 4:3, all objects fully in frame occupying 75% of canvas, generous clear margins; only soft neutral contact shadows.
Genuinely transparent alpha background, suitable over white and charcoal webpages.
No words, text, letters, numbers, logos, captions, people, robots, brains, sparkles, chrome, glitter, blue, glow, neon, bloom, glossy clay, glass, gradients as backdrop, or pedestal.
```
